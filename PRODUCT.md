# PRODUCT.md — The Roots

> Context file for design work (impeccable / design-flow). What this product is,
> who it serves, and the principles every screen must honor.

**register: product** — the design serves the product (an app), it is not the product itself.

---

## Product purpose

The Roots helps newly-arrived immigrants navigate the practical work of settling
into life in the United States — phone, bank account, housing, SSN/ITIN,
healthcare — as a guided, structured **Journey** instead of a pile of
intimidating, scattered bureaucracy.

The core promise: **"You are not lost. Here is the next step."**

## Who it serves

- **Primary user:** a recent immigrant (often anxious, often not a native English
  speaker) in their first weeks-to-months in the US, facing high-stakes,
  unfamiliar systems.
- **Mindset:** stressed, time-poor, wary of scams, unsure who to trust. Not
  looking to play a game — looking to not make a costly mistake.
- **Languages:** bilingual-first. English is rarely their first language. Content
  must survive translation and support non-Latin scripts (中文, العربية, etc.).

## Tone

Warm, calm, unhurried, trustworthy. A patient guide, not a cheerful mascot and
not a cold government form. Plain language over jargon. Reassurance over urgency.

The feeling target, in Dianne's words: **溫暖沉靜 (warm and calm)**.

## Strategic principles

1. **Trust > engagement mechanics.** Never lock content. Every quest is always
   readable and previewable. Four states (Done · In Progress · Available ·
   Recommended Next) — never "locked/unlocked." Restriction creates anxiety;
   trust creates retention.
2. **Journey is the spine.** All structured content lives in Journey. Guide
   (who to ask), Kit (emergency tools), and Circles (community wisdom) are
   service layers that exist to make a quest walkable. Home is a curated view
   that owns nothing.
3. **Home shows the next step in 0 seconds.** Home's only job is "where am I /
   what's next." It shows at most **3 things + 1 progress indicator**. Anything
   more violates its role.
4. **Kit (emergency) is never hidden.** It can save someone in a high-stress
   moment, so it is always reachable.
5. **Personalization is invisible infrastructure.** Profile (visa status,
   language, state, arrival date) feeds every other module but shows the user
   nothing on its own.

## Anti-references (what to avoid)

- Gamified habit apps with streaks, locked levels, and "unlocked!" confetti.
- Cold, dense government / legal portals (walls of grey text and form fields).
- Trendy-but-busy aesthetics (heavy glassmorphism, neon, dark dashboards) that
  feel cool but raise cognitive load.
- AI-slop hallmarks: identical card grids, side-stripe accent borders,
  hero-metric templates, restated headings.
